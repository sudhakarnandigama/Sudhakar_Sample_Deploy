# ADR-005: Model book–author as many-to-many

- **Date:** 2026-08-21
- **Status:** Accepted

## Context

A book can have multiple authors and an author can write multiple books.

## Decision

Use a bidirectional many-to-many association materialized by the `book_author` join table, with `Book` as the owning side. No `CascadeType.REMOVE` on the authors collection.

## Consequences

- Correctly models real-world authorship.
- Deleting an author severs join-table rows without deleting books.
- Join-table inserts/deletes are controlled by the owning side (`Book`).
